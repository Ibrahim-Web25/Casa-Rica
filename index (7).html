Cuisines on one menu

Set within Central Park in DHA Phase II, Casa Rica gathers steaks, seafood, pasta, pizza and all-day breakfast under one warm, low-lit roof. Velvet banquettes, brass detailing and quiet corners make the room feel closer to a private lounge than a restaurant.

Guests come for long lunches that drift into evening, for hi-tea with friends, and for the kind of dinner that isn't rushed — service that reads the table and a kitchen that plates with real care.

THE ART OF DINING

Presentation is restraint — never more on the plate than the dish needs.

Every dish leaves the pass once, plated with intention: negative space, a single sauce line, height where it earns its place. Nothing arrives twice, and nothing arrives unfinished.

CRAFT

A kitchen that treats
twelve cuisines as one discipline.

Continental, Chinese, Thai and desi sections run side by side in the same kitchen, held to the same standard: proper stock, proper sear, proper rest. It's a wide menu built by cooks who specialise, not a single kitchen trying to do everything at once.
